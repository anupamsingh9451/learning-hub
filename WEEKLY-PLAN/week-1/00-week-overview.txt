6. AI SKILL OF THE WEEK – STRUCTURED PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------
Is hafte AI se code LIKHWANA nahi hai, sirf REVIEW karwana hai. Achha prompt
5 hisson me hota hai:

  ROLE        – AI kaun hai (e.g. senior PHP reviewer)
  TASK        – kya karna hai (review karna)
  CONTEXT     – background (PHP 8.3, learning exercise, no framework)
  CONSTRAINTS – hadd (code rewrite mat karo, max 5 points)
  ACCEPTANCE  – jawab kaisa dikhna chahiye (problem, kyun, chhota fix, score)

Flow: Tum code likho  ->  AI review kare  ->  FAISLA TUM karo (accept/reject).

=== Copy-paste wala review prompt ===
ROLE: You are a senior PHP reviewer.
TASK: Review the code below for OOP design, SOLID and error handling.
CONTEXT: PHP 8.3+, strict_types, a Week 1 learning exercise, no framework.
CONSTRAINTS: Do not rewrite the code. At most 5 points, most important first.
  Quote the line each point refers to. Say so when you are unsure.
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA: Each point has the problem, why it matters and the smallest
  fix. End with a score out of 10 and one thing done well.

[yahan apna code paste karo]
=====================================

Ye prompt English me hi rakhna – AI ke liye clear rehta hai.
