4. SKILL 1 – VIDEO EDITING
------------------------------------------------------------
    Kya hai : Reels, YouTube, ads ko kaat-jod ke, captions laga ke dekhne
              layak banana. Har creator, coach, dukaan ko video chahiye,
              time nahi hai.
    Kiske liye: Jinhe akele baith ke creative kaam pasand. Phone se shuru.
    Kya seekhna:
              - Cut aur pace (video boring na lage)
              - Captions/text (saaf, sahi time pe)
              - Sound (saaf awaaz, music, effects)
              - Hook (pehle 3 second me rok lo)
              - Colour aur B-roll (professional look)
    Free me : CapCut (phone + laptop)   https://www.capcut.com
              DaVinci Resolve (laptop)  https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve
              YouTube: "CapCut tutorial for beginners", "DaVinci Resolve beginner"
    90 din  : Mahina 1 – ek software, roz 1 chhoti edit (30 din = 30 edits)
              Mahina 2 – 5 pasand ke creators jaisi reels, 10 best ka portfolio
              Mahina 3 – 3 chhote creators/local business ke liye 1-1 free
                         video, review lo, phir paise maango
    Pehla paisa:
              - 5k-50k followers wale creators ko DM, unki hi ek reel dobara
                edit karke bhejo
              - Shuru me kam rate, par time pe kaam. 3 khush clients ke baad
                rate badhao
              - Mahine ka package (jaise 15 reels) sabse stable
    AI ka role: Captions, rough cut AI bana deta hai. Kahani aur hook ki
              samajh tumhe alag karegi.
    Badi galti: Mahino tak sirf tutorials, kisi ke liye ek bhi video nahi.


------------------------------------------------------------
5. SKILL 2 – DIGITAL MARKETING
------------------------------------------------------------
    Kya hai : Instagram, Google, WhatsApp se business ke liye customers
              laana: ads, content plan, aur paisa kahan sahi lag raha hai.
    Kiske liye: Jinhe "log kyun khareedte hain" sochna, aur numbers +
              creativity dono theek lagte hain.
    Kya seekhna:
              - Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook)
              - Google Ads + SEO
              - Content (kya, kab, kyun post)
              - Copywriting (click karwane wale words)
              - Numbers padhna (kharch vs customers)
    Free me : Google Skillshop      https://skillshop.withgoogle.com
              Meta Blueprint        https://www.facebook.com/business/learn
                                    (course free, exam ke paise)
              HubSpot Academy       https://academy.hubspot.com
              Semrush Academy (SEO) https://www.semrush.com/academy/
    90 din  : Mahina 1 – Skillshop + HubSpot ka ek-ek course, roz ek brand ka
                         page dekh ke likho woh kya sahi kar raha hai
              Mahina 2 – apna/dost ke business ka page 30 din chalao, chhote
                         budget ka ek ad
              Mahina 3 – result + seekh ek page me = case study, local
                         businesses ko dikhao
    Pehla paisa:
              - Gym, salon, coaching, clinic, dukaan – inke paas online koi
                nahi hota
              - Pehle mahine kam fees, par result dikhao
              - Job: "Digital Marketing Executive", "Performance Marketing Intern"
    AI ka role: Ad copy, post ideas AI deta hai. Kis customer ko kya
              dikhana hai, yeh insaan ka faisla.
    Badi galti: Sirf certificates jama karna. Client result dekhta hai.

6. SKILL 3 – DATA ANALYTICS
------------------------------------------------------------
    Kya hai : Company ke data (sales, customers, kharch) ko saaf karke
              batana kya ho raha hai aur aage kya karein. Excel, SQL,
              dashboards.
    Kiske liye: Hisaab-kitaab, pattern, paheli pasand. Laptop zaroori.
    Kya seekhna:
              - Excel (formulas, pivot table, charts) – shuruaat yahin se
              - SQL (bade data se kaam ka data nikalna)
              - Power BI (ek nazar wala dashboard)
              - Basic statistics (average, %, growth, trend)
              - Kahani batana (nateeja simple words me)
    Free me : Microsoft Learn (Excel, Power BI)  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/
              Kaggle Learn (SQL, Python + data)  https://www.kaggle.com/learn
              freeCodeCamp                       https://www.freecodecamp.org
              data.gov.in (asli sarkari data)    https://www.data.gov.in
    90 din  : Mahina 1 – Excel pakka, roz formulas + pivot practice
              Mahina 2 – SQL (Kaggle), phir Power BI ka pehla dashboard
              Mahina 3 – asli data pe 3 projects (cricket, mausam, sales),
                         LinkedIn pe daalo
    Pehla paisa:
              - Job: "Data Analyst Intern", "MIS Executive", "Junior Analyst"
                (MIS me entry aasaan)
              - Chhote business ki sales sheet/dashboard bhi paid kaam
              - Paisa thoda der se, par salary aage achhi badhti hai
    AI ka role: Formula aur SQL likhne me madad. Sahi sawaal aur jawab
              jaanchna tumhara kaam.
    Badi galti: Seedha Python/ML pe koodna. Pehle Excel aur SQL.

7. SKILL 4 – TECH SALES
------------------------------------------------------------
    Kya hai : Software companies ke liye customers dhoondhna. Door-to-door
              nahi – entry level (SDR) email, call, LinkedIn se leads
              banata hai. Fixed salary + incentive.
    Kiske liye: Baat karna achha lage, "nahi" sun ke bura na lage, kamai
              apne kaam se badhani ho.
    Kya seekhna:
              - Sahi customer dhoondhna (kise zaroorat hai)
              - Pehli baat: cold email, call, LinkedIn message
              - Sawaal poochh ke asli problem nikalna
              - "Mehenga hai", "baad me" ka jawab
              - Follow-up (zyada deals yahin band hoti hain)
    Free me : HubSpot Academy (sales courses)  https://academy.hubspot.com
              HubSpot CRM (free)                https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm
              YouTube: "cold calling for SDR", "cold email examples"
              AI chatbot ko customer bana ke roz role play
    90 din  : Mahina 1 – HubSpot sales course, ek product ke 30 sahi
                         customers ki list, 20 cold emails
              Mahina 2 – roz 1 mock call record, ek chhote business ke liye
                         sach me customers dhoondho
              Mahina 3 – list + emails + recording ek jagah, roz 10 jobs pe
                         apply + manager ko seedha message
    Pehla paisa:
              - Job: "SDR", "BDR", "Inside Sales", "Business Development Executive"
              - Chhote business ke liye commission pe customers
              - Zero salary ya "job ke liye paise do" wali jagah se door
    AI ka role: List aur email draft AI. Bharosa jeetna insaan ka kaam.
    Badi galti: Sochna sales = achha bolna. Asli kaam = achha sunna + roz
              follow-up.

8. SKILL 5 – AI AUTOMATION
------------------------------------------------------------
    Kya hai : Bina (ya kam) coding ke apps jodna taaki kaam apne aap ho.
              Jaise form bharte hi WhatsApp jaaye, sheet update ho, AI
              jawab ka draft likhe.
    Kiske liye: Naye tools aazmana, "yeh aasaan kaise ho" sochna pasand.
              Laptop zaroori.
    Kya seekhna:
              - Prompting (AI ko saaf instructions)
              - Automation tools (app ko app se jodna)
              - Google Sheets/Forms (chhote business inhi pe chalte hain)
              - Business samajhna (kaunsa kaam roz repeat hota hai)
              - Jaanchna (automation galti na kare)
    Free me : Make     https://www.make.com
              Zapier   https://zapier.com
              n8n      https://n8n.io   (thoda advanced, self-host bhi)
              YouTube: "Make.com tutorial for beginners", "n8n beginner"
    90 din  : Mahina 1 – roz AI chatbot use, Make/Zapier pe 10 chhote
                         automation (form -> sheet, sheet -> email)
              Mahina 2 – asli problems: enquiry ka turant jawab, appointment
                         reminder, bill ki list
              Mahina 3 – 3 automation ki screen recording, ek local business
                         ke liye ek free me lagao
    Pehla paisa:
              - Coaching, clinic, real estate agent, online dukaan (roz same
                kaam)
              - Setup fee + mahine ki maintenance fee
              - Marketing ya sales job me bhi tumhe alag banati hai
    AI ka role: Yahan AI hi auzaar hai. Tools badlenge, problem samajhna
              aur sahi hal jodna skill hai.
    Badi galti: Tools ki ginti badhana. Client ko tool nahi, bacha hua
              time chahiye.

11. TUMHARE LIYE – DEVELOPER ANGLE (MERA JODA)
------------------------------------------------------------
    PDF beginners ke liye hai. Tum 7+ saal ke Laravel/React developer ho,
    toh in skills ko seedha side income ya AI career me jod sakte ho:

    AI Automation (sabse achha fit):
    - Make/Zapier ki jagah n8n self-host karo (Docker), jo baaki log nahi
      kar paate.
    - n8n ke Webhook node se apne Laravel app ko jodo: form/enquiry ->
      Laravel API -> Claude se draft -> WhatsApp/email.
    - Jahan no-code atke, wahan custom code (Code node ya chhota Laravel
      endpoint). Yahi tumhara "edge" hai.
    - Yeh tumhare AI Developer goal ke saath seedha milta hai.

    Data Analytics:
    - SQL tumhe aata hai. Power BI/Metabase dashboards + client ke MySQL
      se reports = jaldi paid kaam.

    Tech Sales / Digital Marketing:
    - Freelancing me client laane ke liye kaam aayenge (cold email,
      case study, follow-up), bhale full-time na karo.

    Rule wahi: ek chuno, 90 din, proof banao.
