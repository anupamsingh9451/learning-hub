Act as a Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist and Senior UI Designer.
Create a high-impact closing Call-to-Action (CTA) section paired with an enterprise Footer system.

Specifications:
1. High-Converting CTA Banner (CTA Gallery style):
   - Vibrant gradient or deep neutral card container with subtle noise overlay.
   - Compelling value-driven headline ("Ship your web apps 10x faster today").
   - Inline action form: Email input with integrated submit button.
   - Risk-reversal microcopy: "No credit card required", "Cancel anytime", "Free 14-day trial".
   - Subtle background glow gradient accentuating the action button.
2. Comprehensive Footer (Footer Design style):
   - Brand column: Logo mark, 1-line elevator pitch, and newsletter subscription field.
   - 4 Navigation columns: Product (Features, Integrations, Pricing, Changelog), Resources (Documentation, Guides, API, Community), Company (About, Careers, Blog), Legal (Privacy, Terms, Security).
   - Trust and Status Row: Live system health pill (e.g. "All Systems Operational" in emerald), localized language/currency selector, and SVG social links.
