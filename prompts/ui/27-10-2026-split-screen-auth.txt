Act as a Senior Frontend Engineer.
Design a high-converting, modern Split-Screen Authentication page supporting both Sign In and Sign Up modes.

Layout Structure:
1. Left Branded Panel (Desktop Only):
   - Deep indigo/slate gradient background with subtle blur glow.
   - App logo and brand tagline.
   - Customer testimonial card with 5-star rating, customer quote, and verified name/title.
2. Right Form Column:
   - Mode switcher: Segmented tab toggle ("Sign In" / "Create Account").
   - One-click social auth: GitHub and Google buttons with official SVG icons.
   - "Or continue with email" divider line.
   - Form fields: Full Name (sign up only), Email Address, and Password.
   - Password field with show/hide eye toggle button.
   - "Forgot password?" link aligned to the right.
   - Submit button with loading spinner state capability.
   - Terms and Privacy policy disclaimer text at the bottom.

Behavior & Validation:
- Provide clean inline error states (red border for invalid email, friendly helper message for password length).
