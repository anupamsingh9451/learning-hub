Act as a Fintech & Payment Integration Specialist.
Implement a secure, production-ready Payment and Subscription billing system.

Project Context:
- Gateway to Use: [Razorpay (India - UPI, Cards, NetBanking) OR Stripe OR Lemon Squeezy]
- Billing Model: [One-time Purchase OR Recurring Monthly/Annual Subscription]
- Plans & Pricing: [e.g. Free Tier, Pro ($29/mo), Enterprise ($99/mo)]

Deliverables to Implement:
1. Checkout Session Flow:
   - Server-side endpoint to generate Checkout Session / Order ID with price, currency, and customer metadata.
   - Client-side checkout modal or redirection with loading states and error handling.
   - Success redirect page (`/checkout/success?session_id=...`) and Cancel handler.
2. Webhook Event Processing (Crucial):
   - Secure webhook receiver endpoint (`/api/webhooks/payment`).
   - Cryptographic signature verification using Webhook Secret.
   - Handle key events:
     * `payment.captured` / `checkout.session.completed` -> Upgrade user plan, issue access.
     * `subscription.updated` -> Update renewal dates and quota tier.
     * `subscription.cancelled` / `payment.failed` -> Mark subscription past_due or revert to free tier.
   - Idempotency check: Store `event_id` in database to prevent processing duplicate webhook deliveries.
3. Customer Portal / Manage Subscription:
   - Provide endpoint to generate Customer Portal session (change plan, update card, cancel subscription, view invoices).
